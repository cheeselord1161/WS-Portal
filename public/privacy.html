Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

At Forkd, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our commit summary tool.

1. Data We Collect

Commit Data: We process the commit messages you paste or fetch via GitHub to generate changelogs. This data is processed in real-time and is not used to train global AI models.

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication UID via Firebase Auth.
  • Usage Data: We track the number of generations per day to enforce plan limits.
  • Payment Data: Handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We do not store credit card details.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide our service, we share specific data with the following trusted partners:

  • Mistral: Commit messages are sent to Mistral models for summarization.
  • Google Firebase: Used for database hosting and authentication.
  • Lemon Squeezy: Our Merchant of Record for all payment processing and tax compliance.
  • Render Used for web hosting and deployment.

3. Data Retention

We retain usage logs for approximately 14 days to provide customer support and prevent abuse. Account data is retained as long as your account is active.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@forkd-ai.com
